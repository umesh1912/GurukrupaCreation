The workshop process

Four stages, one frame at a time

Every garment moves through the same four hands-on stages before it leaves Surat. Nothing here is automated — the sequence is the craft.

01

Sketch

The embroidery pattern is drawn to the garment's exact panel size before a single bead is threaded, so motifs land correctly at the seam.

02

Thread & bead

Zari, resham, cutdana, sequins and pearls are matched to the base fabric's weight and drape, not just its colour.

03

Hand embroidery

Aari hook and hand-needle work by artisans at the frame, several with more than two decades on the same craft.

04

Finish & press

Thread ends are secured from the reverse side, the garment is steam-pressed, and every panel is checked against the original sketch.

Custom & bulk orders

Tell us the occasion, we'll tell you what it takes

Single bridal pieces and 500-metre export runs go through the same intake form — the workshop just schedules them differently.

  • Reference photo or sketch reviewed within 2 working days
  • Sample panel stitched before full production on bulk orders
  • Standard turnaround: 3–5 weeks for single garments, longer for bulk
  • Fabric can be supplied by you or sourced by the workshop
